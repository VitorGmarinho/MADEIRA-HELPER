ABOUT MADEIRA HELPER

Local help, with a human touch.

Madeira Helper was created to make everyday life easier for residents, newcomers and visitors who need a little help finding the right local service or communicating with Portuguese businesses.

MEET VITOR MARINHO
👋

Your local connection in Madeira.

English-speaking help, local knowledge and a simple way to get things done.

MY STORY

Helping people feel more at home in Madeira.

My name is Vitor. After spending many years living and working in London, I eventually made Madeira my home.

Living somewhere new can be exciting, but everyday life can also become complicated when you don’t know who to contact, how local systems work, or how to communicate with a business in Portuguese.

I understand how important it is to have someone who can point you in the right direction.

That’s why I created Madeira Helper.

The idea is simple: tell me what you need and I’ll help you find the right local service, business or next step. Where possible, I can also help bridge the communication gap between English-speaking customers and Portuguese businesses.
