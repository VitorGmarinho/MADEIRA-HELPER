QUESTIONS

Before you apply.

Does it cost anything to join?

There is currently no membership charge to join the Founding Business network during the Madeira Helper launch phase.

Does applying guarantee a listing?

No. Madeira Helper reviews applications before deciding whether a business is appropriate for the directory and network.

Do you take commission?

Madeira Helper does not currently take a commission simply because a customer discovers a Founding Business through the launch network.

Will there be paid plans later?

Madeira Helper may introduce optional Partner plans, additional promotion and other business services in the future. Businesses will be informed before joining any paid service.
